STARDIG // PRIVACY

LAST UPDATED 23 OCTOBER 2026

WHAT THIS IS

Stardig (formerly Sound Cosmos) is a map of electronic music by hellomynameisdruc. Music plays through YouTube's own embedded player. You can use the map without an account; an account is only needed to chat in COMMS and to broadcast to other pilots.

WHAT WE KEEP

  • Your email address, to sign you in (by a link sent to your email, or through Google sign-in). From Google we receive only your email and basic profile; we do not use your Google name or picture.
  • Your call sign, portrait number and rank, shown to other pilots.
  • The messages you send to the open COMMS channel, and reports you make about messages.

Messages in a fleet's channel, and what you broadcast, are passed live between the pilots in it and are not stored.

Your beacons, flight log and settings are kept only in your own browser (local storage) and never leave it.

WHERE IT LIVES

Accounts and messages are stored with Supabase (servers in the EU, Frankfurt). The site itself is served by GitHub Pages. Videos are played by YouTube, under Google's privacy policy. We do not sell or share your data, and we run no ads or trackers.

YOUR CONTROL

You can change your call sign and portrait at any time, delete your own messages, and delete your account in your pilot profile: that removes your email, profile and messages for good.
